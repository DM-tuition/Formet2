Delivery & returns

Every piece is made to order: about 7–10 working days to make, then 6–8 days to reach us in Manchester before it’s dispatched to you. Allow about three to four weeks in the UK, and up to six weeks further afield. You’ll see an estimated date range before you place your order.

RegionAfter dispatchPrice

Orders outside the UK may be charged import duties, paid by the customer. Because every piece is made for you, we can’t accept change-of-mind returns; your 14‑day statutory right to cancel still applies. If something arrives faulty or incorrect, email formetclo.info@gmail.com with your order number and photos.

Care

Wash inside out at 30°C on a gentle cycle, with similar colours. No fabric softener. Do not tumble dry.
