# formet — version 2

The second design study for **formet**, the Manchester knitwear label. It builds on
[version 1](https://github.com/DM-tuition/Formet-) and adds the navy No. 1 Raglan.

**Live:** https://dm-tuition.github.io/Formet2/

- **No. 1:** the back print (FORMET / is the / 1 / MCR) is set as type cut out of the page, so the
  photo shows only through the letters. Scrolling dives through the 1 into the photo.
- **A glass lens (WebGL)** follows the pointer and sees through the type to the photo; after the
  dive the photo ripples with scroll speed. Tap the type on a phone or tablet to see it.
- **The rail:** the garment hangs and swings on a spring. Brush past it, swipe it or tap it.
- **Day/night:** night falls as a circle grown from the switch.
- **Studio collage:** the owner's studio shots, layered at different depths.

This is not the official store. The bag and checkout are a demo: nothing is sold and no card
details are asked for. Photos are the brand's own.
